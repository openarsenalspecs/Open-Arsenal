# Governance Specifications

The AI Governance Specifications establish a foundation for creating artificial intelligence systems that are transparent, accountable, secure, and aligned with human oversight. They provide structured approaches for managing AI behavior, defining operational boundaries, enforcing policies, and maintaining visibility into how intelligent systems make decisions and perform actions.

These specifications address key challenges in responsible AI deployment, including safety assessment, risk management, compliance, provenance, explainability, validation, and controlled autonomy. By incorporating governance mechanisms throughout the AI lifecycle, they help organizations build systems that can be monitored, evaluated, and trusted while preserving human authority over critical decisions.

Designed as modular and interoperable building blocks, these governance frameworks support a wide range of AI environments, from individual agents to complex multi-agent systems. They enable developers, organizations, and communities to create AI solutions that prioritize accountability, verifiability, and long-term sustainability while reducing uncertainty in increasingly autonomous technology ecosystems.
