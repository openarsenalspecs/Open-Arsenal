# Reasoning Specifications

This collection of reasoning specifications provides a comprehensive framework for building AI systems that can analyze ethical dimensions, generate and evaluate multiple interpretations, explain their decisions, and maintain full transparency throughout the reasoning process. By combining structured ethical representations, human oversight, auditability, adaptive runtimes, and modular cognitive workflows, these specifications enable the development of intelligent systems that are interpretable, accountable, and capable of producing trustworthy and verifiable reasoning outcomes.

All specifications are released under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+) and may be freely used with the required attribution provisions outlined in Section 7 of the license. Organizations seeking attribution-free commercial deployment may obtain a Specification Branding License, with pricing based on the specification category, deployment scale, and the size of the environment in which it is implemented.

## Reasoning Specs  

- **Audit and Traceability Layer**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/audit-and-traceability-layer/](https://roxanneardary.com/audit-and-traceability-layer/)    
Tracks and verifies all steps in AI moral reasoning and narrative generation with full provenance.

- **Cognitive Orchestration Stack**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/cognitive-orchestration-stack/](https://roxanneardary.com/cognitive-orchestration-stack/)  
Modular framework that separates reasoning from generation using structured cognitive workflows.

- **Ethics and Neutrality Constraints**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/ethics-and-neutrality-constraints/](https://roxanneardary.com/ethics-and-neutrality-constraints/)    
Governance rules separating ethical inference, interpretation, and generation to keep AI reasoning traceable and neutral.

- **Ethics Normalization Layer**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/ethics-normalization-layer/](https://roxanneardary.com/ethics-normalization-layer/)  
Transforms moral interpretations into structured, neutral ethical representations with theme labels, summaries, and confidence scores for reasoning systems.

- **Horizon Runtime**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/horizon-runtime/](https://roxanneardary.com/horizon-runtime/)  
Universal AI runtime supporting adaptive inference and reasoning models.

- **Human-in-the-Loop Moral Approval Gate**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/human-in-the-loop-moral-approval-gate/](https://roxanneardary.com/human-in-the-loop-moral-approval-gate/)    
Requires human review and approval of AI-generated moral interpretations before use in downstream processes.

- **Moral Inference Engine**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/moral-inference-engine/](https://roxanneardary.com/moral-inference-engine/)  
Open modular framework for AI systems that analyze moral dimensions of texts, generate multiple interpretations, rank them with confidence scores, and provide transparent explanations while preserving interpretive diversity.

- **Moral Ranking and Diversity System**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/moral-ranking-and-diversity-system/](https://roxanneardary.com/moral-ranking-and-diversity-system/)  
Framework for evaluating and ranking multiple moral interpretations from AI reasoning systems, with confidence scoring, diversity preservation, and transparent explanations.

- **Story Generation Engine**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/story-generation-engine/](https://roxanneardary.com/story-generation-engine/)  
Generates narratives from approved moral interpretations with built-in alignment validation.

- **Transparency and Explainability Layer**    
[https://roxanneardary.com/transparency-and-explainability-layer/](https://roxanneardary.com/transparency-and-explainability-layer/)    
Methods for explaining AI moral reasoning decisions, confidence visualization, ambiguity detection, and uncertainty reporting.
