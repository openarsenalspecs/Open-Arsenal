# Policy Relationship Analysis Specifications

The Policy Relationship Analysis specifications provide a structured framework for understanding how policies connect to laws, institutions, organizations, public officials, funding, programs, and decisions. Rather than treating policy as an isolated document or action, these specifications model the broader network of relationships surrounding policy development, implementation, enforcement, and outcomes.

Key capabilities include relationship mapping, entity resolution, legislative and regulatory analysis, evidence tracking, provenance, policy comparison, influence detection, and accountability analysis. The specifications can connect legislation to funding and programs, organizations to government activity, public commitments to subsequent actions, and institutional decisions to the entities and evidence associated with them. This creates a clearer picture of how individual policy elements interact across complex systems.

The specifications are designed to support transparent, evidence-based, and auditable policy analysis. By preserving relationships between entities, decisions, evidence, legal authorities, and outcomes, they can help identify conflicts of interest, deviations from stated positions, organizational influence, regulatory dependencies, and connections between public resources and private interests. The resulting relationship networks provide a foundation for deeper governance, compliance, oversight, and public accountability analysis.

## Policy Relationship Analysis Specs

**ActionCheck**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/actioncheck/](https://roxanneardary.com/actioncheck/)  
Connects campaign promises, policy positions, voting records, legislative actions, financial disclosures, and organizational relationships to identify deviations between stated policy commitments and actual governing behavior.

**CodeClear**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/codeclear/](https://roxanneardary.com/codeclear/)  
Maps relationships among laws, statutes, amendments, regulations, funding mechanisms, and implementation requirements, allowing policy and legislative changes to be analyzed as interconnected systems.

**FarmWeb Intelligence**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/farmweb-intelligence/](https://roxanneardary.com/farmweb-intelligence/)  
Connects agricultural organizations, ownership structures, government programs, legislation, public funding, and policy-related activity to reveal relationships between agricultural interests and government resources.

**Financial Accountability**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/financial-accountability/](https://roxanneardary.com/financial-accountability/)  
Analyzes relationships among public finances, government spending, financial activity, organizations, funding sources, and accountability records to identify patterns and connections that may affect public policy and institutional decision-making.

**Integrity & Deviation Detection**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/integrity-and-deviation-detection/](https://roxanneardary.com/integrity-and-deviation-detection/)  
Analyzes public decisions, stated commitments, legislative behavior, affiliated interests, and influence indicators to identify deviations and potential relationship-based influence patterns.

**Open Intelligence Stack**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/open-intelligence-stack/](https://roxanneardary.com/open-intelligence-stack/)  
Provides a governance-oriented intelligence architecture connecting policy, law, compliance, authority, evidence, provenance, entities, and decisions so relationships surrounding policy and institutional decisions can be analyzed and audited.

**Relationship Mapping**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/relationship-mapping/](https://roxanneardary.com/relationship-mapping/)  
Maps relationships among people, organizations, corporations, government entities, financial interests, events, and other entities to expose networks relevant to policy, governance, influence, and accountability analysis.

**Veridion System**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/veridion-system/](https://roxanneardary.com/veridion-system/)  
Connects events, entities, evidence, sources, policies, and decisions while evaluating relationships, corroboration, reliability, and temporal patterns to support evidence-based policy and intelligence analysis.

**VeritasChain**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/veritaschain/](https://roxanneardary.com/veritaschain/)  
Connects legal, economic, historical, and institutional data through evidence-based causal intelligence, allowing relationships between decisions, institutions, policies, and their consequences to be traced and analyzed.

**VeritasCode**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/veritascode/](https://roxanneardary.com/veritascode/)  
Analyzes laws across jurisdictions to identify conflicts, constitutional questions, and legal inconsistencies, creating a relationship layer between statutes and other legal authorities for comparative policy and legislative analysis.

**VerifyRights**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/verifyrights/](https://roxanneardary.com/verifyrights/)  
Maps corporate relationships such as parent companies, subsidiaries, acquisitions, and affiliated organizations and connects them to licensing terms, authorized use, compliance, and contractual obligations.

**Zentis**  
[https://roxanneardary.com/zentis/](https://roxanneardary.com/zentis/)  
Provides infrastructure for interpretive governance by connecting policy analysis, governance rules, legal constraints, and legal-risk modeling while maintaining transparent and explainable outputs.
