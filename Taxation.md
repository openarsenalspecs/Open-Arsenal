# Taxation Specifications

These open source taxation specifications provide a comprehensive framework for modernizing tax administration, public finance, and personal financial management through transparent, AI-assisted systems. They cover resource-backed public dividend models, government budgeting, tax planning, automated compliance, and financial forecasting while emphasizing privacy, accountability, and verifiable financial records. Designed with modular architectures, the specifications can be deployed independently or integrated into broader civic technology and financial ecosystems.

Key features include AI-powered tax guidance, automated tax allocation and compliance workflows, transparent public budgeting, citizen oversight, encrypted financial assistance, dividend distribution frameworks, surplus revenue management, long-term financial forecasting, and auditable governance processes. Together, they promote efficient public administration, responsible fiscal management, and accessible financial tools while remaining open, extensible, and adaptable to organizations of any size.

Organizations seeking attribution-free commercial deployment may obtain a Specification Branding License, with pricing based on the specification category, deployment scale, and the size of the environment in which it is implemented.
