# Education Specifications

The Education Specifications Bundle provides a comprehensive foundation for building open, intelligent, and accessible learning ecosystems. It brings together specifications focused on adaptive tutoring, personalized education, career planning, technical instruction, creative skill development, and experiential learning. Through AI-powered guidance, simulations, interactive visualization, and data-driven feedback, these systems help learners progress at their own pace while connecting knowledge with practical applications.

Designed for schools, independent learners, educators, training organizations, and professional development environments, the bundle supports a future where education is more flexible, affordable, and widely available. By combining traditional learning approaches with emerging technologies, these specifications create opportunities for deeper understanding, hands-on exploration, and lifelong skill development across academic, vocational, and creative disciplines.

All specifications are released under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+) and may be used free of charge, provided the attribution requirements under Section 7 of the license are maintained. Organizations requiring attribution-free deployment may obtain a Specification Branding License (SBL), with licensing fees based on the specification type, deployment scope, and the size of the network or environment in which the specification is implemented.

  [https://roxanneardary.com/continuum-companion/](https://roxanneardary.com/continuum-companion/)

- **LibreSpaceflight**  
  [https://roxanneardary.com/librespaceflight/](https://roxanneardary.com/librespaceflight/)  
  Open platform for democratizing spaceflight knowledge. Converts aerospace concepts into interactive, testable simulations for active learning, teaching orbital mechanics, robotics, spacecraft systems, and engineering principles.
