# Game Specifications

The Gaming Specifications Bundle is a comprehensive collection of open specifications focused on the technologies and systems that power modern interactive experiences. It covers game engines, virtual worlds, multiplayer architectures, artificial intelligence, procedural generation, cinematic rendering, simulation systems, and creator-centric platforms. Designed around modularity and interoperability, the bundle provides reusable frameworks that help developers build scalable, extensible, and community-driven applications.

By promoting open standards and specification-driven development, the bundle enables the creation of persistent digital worlds that can evolve over time and integrate with a broader ecosystem of tools and services. Its specifications support entertainment, education, simulation, and creative experimentation while encouraging collaboration, transparency, and user ownership. The result is a flexible foundation for building next-generation gaming experiences that combine storytelling, simulation, and emerging technologies.

All specifications are released under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+) and may be used free of charge, provided the attribution requirements under Section 7 of the license are maintained. Organizations requiring attribution-free deployment may obtain a Specification Branding License (SBL), with licensing fees based on the specification type, deployment scope, and the size of the network or environment in which the specification is implemented.
