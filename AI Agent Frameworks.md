# AI Agent Frameworks

The Open Arsenal AI Agent Framework specifications form a comprehensive, modular ecosystem for designing, building, deploying, governing, and continuously improving intelligent AI agents and autonomous systems. Together, these specifications address the essential layers of an advanced agent architecture, including model training and validation, inference and runtime environments, reasoning and cognitive orchestration, persistent memory, knowledge management, multi-agent coordination, workflow automation, and reusable AI experiences. They provide the infrastructure for agents to operate across local, enterprise, cloud, distributed, and air-gapped environments while supporting interoperability, scalability, adaptability, and efficient resource utilization. Through modular components such as Horizon Framework, Horizon Runtime, Cognitive Orchestration Stack, CivicOS AI, LatticeOS, and Commons Reasoning Network, developers and organizations can assemble specialized agent systems, coordinate distributed intelligence, share knowledge, and build increasingly capable autonomous workflows.

Equally important, the ecosystem places governance, transparency, reliability, and human oversight at the center of agent development and execution. Specifications such as AuthenticAI, AuthorityCore, GovernanceFabric, HelmOS, and Layered Provenance Framework establish mechanisms for policy enforcement, accountability, intent verification, evidence-based reasoning, auditability, and verifiable attribution. Supporting specifications provide persistent memory, continuous evaluation, automated testing, model drift detection, explainable deployment, and ongoing improvement throughout the AI lifecycle. AgentBlocks and Knowledge Continuum extend the ecosystem with reusable AI experiences, modular knowledge resources, and opportunities for sharing, licensing, and distribution. Designed as complementary, independently deployable open-source specifications, the collection provides a foundation for building interoperable, trustworthy, and extensible AI agent infrastructure, with human-in-the-loop governance and AGPL-3.0+ principles supporting an open and collaborative development model.

All specifications are released under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+)** and may be freely used, modified, and distributed with the required attribution provisions outlined in **Section 7 of the license**. Organizations seeking attribution-free commercial deployment may obtain a **Specification Branding License (SBL)**, with licensing terms and pricing based on the specification category, deployment scope, usage scale, and the size of the network or organization utilizing the specification.

### Specification FAQs

**The Open Arsenal specifications are designed as foundations, not disposable products.** They define reusable architectures that can be implemented, extended, combined, modified, and adapted to changing technologies and real-world requirements. Modular design allows individual capabilities to evolve without forcing an entire system to be replaced, while vendor-neutral architecture helps preserve independence and interoperability.

**Long-term infrastructure requires long-term thinking.** Open-source licensing encourages examination, reuse, modification, and collaboration, while perpetual commercial licensing can provide organizations with lasting rights to deploy a specification without making foundational infrastructure dependent on recurring licensing renewals. The objective is to create technological foundations that can remain useful as companies, technologies, platforms, and business models change.
