# Municpal Bundle Specifications

This collection of open specifications provides a modular foundation for modern municipal finance, budgeting, procurement oversight, fiscal transparency, and public accountability. Together, they enable governments, civic organizations, and developers to build interoperable systems for budget planning, expenditure tracking, financial disclosures, audit trails, AI-powered analysis, governance workflows, and historical data management. Designed around open standards and transparent architectures, these specifications can be combined to create scalable, vendor-neutral civic technology platforms that improve public trust, streamline financial operations, and make government decision-making more transparent and accountable.

All specifications are released under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+) and may be freely used with the required attribution provisions outlined in Section 7 of the license. Organizations seeking attribution-free commercial deployment may obtain a Specification Branding License, with pricing based on the specification category, deployment scale, and the size of the environment in which it is implemented.  

## Municipal Specs  

- **ActionCheck**  
  [https://roxanneardary.com/actioncheck/](https://roxanneardary.com/actioncheck/)  
  An open-source civic accountability platform that tracks campaign promises, voting records, financial disclosures, and conflicts of interest to help citizens evaluate elected officials.


- **Admin and Moderator Tools**  
  [https://roxanneardary.com/admin-and-moderator-tools/](https://roxanneardary.com/admin-and-moderator-tools/)  
  A governance and administration framework for reviewing, validating, moderating, and approving submissions within civic transparency platforms, including financial and budget-related data.


- **AI Intelligence Engine**  
  [https://roxanneardary.com/ai-intelligence-engine/](https://roxanneardary.com/ai-intelligence-engine/)  
  An analytical AI engine for processing government records, legislation, voting histories, public documents, and financial data, enabling budget tracking, performance analysis, and policy insights.


- **Audit and Traceability Layer**  
  [https://roxanneardary.com/audit-and-traceability-layer/](https://roxanneardary.com/audit-and-traceability-layer/)  
  A provenance and immutable logging framework that records data lineage and AI transformations, ensuring transparency, accountability, and traceability for financial reporting and civic systems.


- **CommonsProtocol**  
  [https://roxanneardary.com/commonsprotocol/](https://roxanneardary.com/commonsprotocol/)  
  An open-source civic transparency platform providing a federated, auditable public ledger for campaign finance, government spending, and civic accountability.


- **Data and Auditability**  
  [https://roxanneardary.com/data-and-auditability/](https://roxanneardary.com/data-and-auditability/)  
  A core framework for verifiable public data with source attribution, audit logs, version histories, and transparent methodologies, providing a trustworthy foundation for budget and financial reporting systems.


- **DividenCity**  
  [https://roxanneardary.com/dividencity/](https://roxanneardary.com/dividencity/)  
  An open-source framework that enables resource-backed Universal Basic Income (UBI) by tracking public resource revenue, surplus budgets, and dividend distribution.


- **FiscalTruth**  
  [https://roxanneardary.com/fiscaltruth/](https://roxanneardary.com/fiscaltruth/)  
  An open-source civic watchdog platform that tracks public spending, detects financial anomalies, and provides transparency into how taxpayer dollars are allocated and spent.


- **Financial Transparency**  
  [https://roxanneardary.com/financial-transparency/](https://roxanneardary.com/financial-transparency/)  
  A framework for managing and publishing public officials' financial disclosures, including assets, transactions, liabilities, and conflicts of interest, supporting oversight of budgeting and public decision-making.


- **Fundamenta**  
  [https://roxanneardary.com/fundamenta/](https://roxanneardary.com/fundamenta/)  
  An open-source, AI-driven government budgeting platform that enables transparent, accountable, and intelligent public financial management for municipalities and public agencies.


- **GovGraph**  
  [https://roxanneardary.com/govgraph/](https://roxanneardary.com/govgraph/)  
  An open graph of public systems that connects campaign finance disclosures, public institutional data, and social media into a unified transparency and analysis framework.


- **KnowledgeContinuum**  
  [https://roxanneardary.com/knowledgecontinuum/](https://roxanneardary.com/knowledgecontinuum/)  
  A temporal knowledge graph framework with entity resolution and versioned data that tracks how fiscal information, budgets, policies, and public records evolve over time while preserving historical context.


- **Orvian**  
  [https://roxanneardary.com/orvian/](https://roxanneardary.com/orvian/)  
  An AI-driven personal financial operating system that organizes money into intelligent budgeting envelopes, savings goals, cash-flow forecasting, and tax automation.
